Room 3

The pine cones

Room 3 is packed with pine cones. Ladybirds squeeze into the gaps to stay warm in winter. That's a fact! To fill this room you'll tell facts from stories and opinions.

Think. A story about a talking ladybird, and a book that tells you how ladybirds really live. How are they different?
Story or true?

Fiction or non-fiction?

Fiction is a made-up story. Non-fiction gives true information. Read each sentence and decide.

Features

What does non-fiction look like?

Non-fiction has parts that help you find information fast: a title, an introduction, headings, pictures with captions and a conclusion.

Fact or opinion

Can you check it?

A fact is true and you can check it. An opinion is what someone thinks or feels. Ask yourself: could I check this in a book?

Read and check

True or false?

Read the text. Then decide if each sentence is true or false. Look back at the text to check.

Germs on Our Hands

Germs are living things, but they are very, very small. You need a microscope to see them.

Germs are almost everywhere. They live on door handles, on toys and on our hands. Some germs are helpful. Others can make us sick.

Washing your hands with soap and warm water gets rid of lots of germs. Scrub for about twenty seconds. That's as long as singing Happy Birthday twice.

When you cough or sneeze, use a tissue or your elbow. Then the germs can't fly across the room.
