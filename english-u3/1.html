Room 1

Welcome to the Bug Hotel

Our class is building a bug hotel, one room at a time. Room 1 is full of hollow bamboo canes, and bees love to sleep inside them. Fill the room with rhymes, sounds and exclamation marks.

Say it out loud. bug, rug, hug. They all end with the same sound, so they rhyme. Poems often use rhymes. You'll find some in the poem Bugs! in your Student's Book.
Rhymes

Which words rhyme?

Say both words out loud. Listen to the end of each word. If the ends sound the same, they rhyme.

Sounds

Same sound, different spelling

Some words sound the same, but the sound is spelled in different ways.

hair · wear · where
One air sound, three spellings: air, ear, ere.
should · could · wood
One short oo sound, two spellings: ould, oo.
In your English book
Workbook page 12. Find the rhyming words, then write two more words with the air sound.
Strong feelings

Exclamation marks

An exclamation mark ! goes at the end of a sentence said with strong feeling: surprise, a shout or a warning.

Ouch! A bee stung me!
Look out, a wasp!
