Room 5

The roof garden

Room 5 is the roof. We plant flowers on top of the hotel, and ants march up and down all day. To finish the hotel you'll add endings to words and put a report in order.

Look. dark, darkness. care, careless. act, action. The letters on the end are called a suffix. They change the word.
-ness and -less

Add -ness or -less

-ness and -less start with a consonant, so the root word stays the same. Watch out: when a word ends in y, the y changes to i.

kind + ness = kindness
being kind
care + less = careless
without care. The e stays.
-ion

Add -ion

When a verb ends in t, adding -ion makes a thing. The t and the ion together say shun.

act + ion = action
