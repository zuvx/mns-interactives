The straw bales

Room 4 is stuffed with straw, and spiders spin their webs between the stalks. To fill it you'll swap words for better words and spin four kinds of sentence.

Which sounds better? "A big spider" or "an enormous spider"? Both mean the same thing. Words that mean nearly the same are called synonyms.
