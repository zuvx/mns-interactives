The log pile

Room 2 is a pile of old logs. Beetles and woodlice hide in the cracks. To fill it you'll build words and sort them, just like stacking logs.

Look closely. grasshopper is two words stuck together: grass + hopper. Can you think of another bug name made of two words?
Compound words

Join two words to make one

A compound word is two words joined together. Write them as one word, with no space.

grass + hopper = grasshopper

Word classes

Nouns, verbs and adjectives

Every word has a job. A noun names something. A verb tells you what is done, or what something is. An adjective describes a noun.

The tiny ant carries a crumb.
Watch out for can and are. They are verbs too: Bees can fly. Spiders are clever.

noun: namingverb: doing or beingadjective: describing

Pronouns

Swap the noun for a pronoun

A pronoun takes the place of a noun, so you don't say the same name again and again.

Mia found a beetle. She put it on a leaf.
She is Mia. it is the beetle.
