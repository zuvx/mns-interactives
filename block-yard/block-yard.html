The Block Yard

Unit 4: adding and subtracting with trading, and finding the load under the tarpaulin.

The yard gate

In this yard every number is built from blocks. A little cube is one. A rod is ten ones joined together. A flat is one hundred. Tap a shelf button to bring a block onto the mat. Tap the pile itself to take one block back.

Key words

onesSingle cubes.
tensA rod of ten ones.
regroupTrade ten ones for one ten, or one ten for ten ones.
estimateA sensible close answer, worked out quickly.
totalWhat you get when you add.
differenceWhat is left when you subtract.
unknownA number we have not been told yet.

Job 1: build 47 on the mat

Bring on 4 tens and 7 ones. The mat counts what you put down.

Bay 1: a load that needs no trading

Not every addition needs a trade. Build the first number, then bring on the second number, and look hard at the ones pile before you decide.

24 + 65

Bay 2: ten ones make one ten

This is the one from your Student's Book. Build 36, bring on 28, and count the ones pile before you trade.

36 + 28

Bay 3: a load too big for the mat

Sometimes ten tens turn up as well. Ten rods are traded for one flat, which is one hundred.

68 + 87

Bay 4: taking a load away

To subtract, build the first number and then take blocks back off the mat. Check the ones first: sometimes there are plenty.

77 − 23

Bay 5: breaking a ten open

When there are not enough ones, break one rod open. Ten ones fall out. The load is worth exactly the same as before.

64 − 35

Bay 6: what is under the tarpaulin?

Part of the load is hidden under a tarpaulin. You know the total and you know one part, so you can work out the part you cannot see. Build your answer on the mat and the tarpaulin will lift.

14 + ? = 20

76 + ? = 100

40 − ? = 23

No blocks this time
