The Block Yard: Unit 5

Bigger loads. Hundreds, tens and ones, all the way to a thousand.

Back at the yard

You know this yard. A cube is one, a rod is ten, a flat is one hundred. This term the loads are bigger, so you will be counting flats as well.

Key words

hundredsA flat is ten rods joined together.
regroupTrade ten ones for a ten, or ten tens for a hundred.
estimateA sensible close answer, worked out quickly.
multiple of tenA number you land on counting in tens: 40, 260, 900.
unknownA number we have not been told yet.

Bay 1: pick your pair

When you add three numbers you can start with any two of them. The total comes out the same every time. So start with the pair that is easiest, and the pair that makes ten is usually the easiest of all.

Bay 5: ten rods make a flat

Now the rods pile is the one to watch. Ten rods are traded for one flat, exactly the way ten ones are traded for one rod. Taking away works the other way: a flat can be broken into ten rods.

385 + 40

460 + 90

438 − 70

Bay 6: whole flats on and off

Adding or taking away a hundreds number only touches the flats. Nothing is traded at all, so check before you reach for the trade button.
