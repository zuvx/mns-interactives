How you get promoted

You start as a Packer. Each lesson you can earn the next level, and you keep every level you earn.

Level 1 · Packer — Lesson 1
Count on and count back in 10s and 100s from any number, including when the count goes past a hundred.
Level 2 · Wrapper — Lesson 2
Tell even from odd, and say how you know without just reciting the rule.
Level 3 · Sorter — Lesson 3
Explain even and odd by sharing between 2, and sort numbers too big to draw.
Level 4 · Inspector — Lesson 4
Give a sensible range for a jar you only glanced at, then group in tens to check.
Level 5 · Ready — Lesson 5
All four of the above, on new numbers, ready for the Progress Check on paper.
About the sweets in your jar. You earn them for solving, never lose them for trying. A hint costs nothing — using one when you are stuck is exactly what it is for. Show your jar to your teacher at the end of the lesson.

The one factory rule

Look at what the numbers do. Do not just chant them.
