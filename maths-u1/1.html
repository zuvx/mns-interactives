Your first shift

Welcome to the packing line!

Sweets come down the belt. Ten sweets fill one tray. Ten trays fill one crate, which is one hundred. Your job is to keep count.

100101
How this works. You work on your own. Every question is yours to work out. Wrong answers are fine — you get a clue, and a full hint after two tries. You collect sweets in your jar to show your teacher at the end.

Three stations, then your badge:

  • Station 1 — trays of ten. Watch which digit moves.
  • Station 2 — crates of a hundred.
  • Station 3 — the tricky corner, where the count goes past a hundred.
Station 1 · Trays of ten

Add a tray, add ten
