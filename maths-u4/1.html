How this works. You work on your own. Every stall asks you a question. You work out the answer and type it in. The souq only tells you if you are right. If you get stuck, try again — a hint appears after two tries.

Stall 3 · The Efficient Trader

Same total — but which is easier?

Both orders give the same answer. A good trader picks the order that is quicker to work out in your head. Choose one, then work out the total yourself.

Abigail says: "To add 13 and 75 I would choose 75 + 13. It is easier to add a small number onto a big one." Do you agree? You will write about this on the next screen.
