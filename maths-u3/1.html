Key words — tap to open, then tap the speaker to hear the word
numeralA number written in digits, like 349.
digitOne of the symbols 0 to 9.
place holderA zero that keeps a column empty, like the 0 in 705.
zeroNothing in that column. We do not say it out loud.
numberHow many there are. We can write it in digits or in words.
Your first day

Welcome to the Sorting Room!

Every parcel needs an address label. Some show digits, some show words. Your job is to write the missing one.

How this works. You work on your own. Every parcel asks you a question and you work out the answer. Wrong answers are fine — you get a clue, and a full hint after two tries. You collect points in your postbag to show your teacher at the end.

Three benches, then your badge:

  • Bench 1 — read the digits, build the label in words.
  • Bench 2 — listen to the address, write the digits.
  • Bench 3 — the Spelling Desk. Some labels are spelt wrongly.
Bench 1 · Build the label

Write the number in words

Tap the word cards in order to build the label. Tap a placed card to take it back.

Listen, then write the numeral

Bench 3 · The Spelling Desk

These labels are spelt wrongly

A new worker wrote these labels. Each one has a spelling mistake. Write it again, spelt correctly.

Watch out: forty has no u. fifty has no v after the f-i. And eight keeps its gh.
