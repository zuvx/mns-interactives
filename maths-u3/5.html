Before the exam

Time to check what you can do

This is practice, not the real test. The numbers here are different from the paper you will do afterwards.

Five desks, five skills. Match numerals to names · words into digits · digits into words · read counters · build a number from its parts. If a desk feels hard, that is the one to practise again before the paper.
