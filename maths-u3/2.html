Scale 3 · The empty column

What happens when a column is empty?

Sometimes there are no tens, or no ones. We still need a digit to hold the column open. That digit is zero — and we never say it out loud.

Careful: "four hundred and six" is 406, not 4006. Hundreds, tens, ones — always three columns.
