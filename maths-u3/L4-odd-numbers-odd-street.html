Today’s round

Welcome to Odd Street

Every house on Odd Street has an odd number. The even ones are on the road opposite.

How to spot them. Look at the last digit only. If it is 1, 3, 5, 7 or 9, the number is odd. A number can have an 8 in it and still be odd: 189 ends in 9, so it is odd.

Three stops, then your badge:

  • Stop 1 — deliver only to the houses on Odd Street.
  • Stop 2 — the round follows a sequence. Work out the next doors.
  • Stop 3 — write the door numbers on the labels, in words and digits.
