Today’s round

Welcome to Even Street

Every house on Even Street has an even number. The odd ones are on the road opposite.

How to spot them. Look at the last digit only. If it is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8, the number is even. Careful with a 0 on the end: 260 is even, because 260 can be shared into two equal groups of 130.

Three stops, then your badge:

  • Stop 1 — deliver only to the houses on Even Street.
  • Stop 2 — the round follows a sequence. Work out the next doors.
