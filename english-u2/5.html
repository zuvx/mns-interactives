The writing desk

One idea, one paragraph

A long message is hard to read in one block. Writers break it into paragraphs. Each paragraph holds one idea. When the idea changes, you start a new one.

How a reader sees it. The white space at the start of a paragraph is a signal: here comes something new. It gives their eyes a rest too.
Spotting the break

Where does the new paragraph start?

Read each pair. Same idea, so same paragraph? Or a new idea, so a new paragraph?

The shape

Put the letter in order

A friendly letter has the same parts every time. Tap one card, tap another, they swap.

Greeting. Dear Gran,
Paragraph 1 — why I am writing. Thank you for the parcel.
Paragraph 2 — my news. What has been happening here.
Paragraph 3 — a question for them. How is the garden?
Ending. Love from Amal
Planning

Plan your own letter

You are writing to somebody you know well. Your plan goes on the planning sheet. These two questions are the ones planners get wrong.

In your English book
Write the letter. Greeting, three paragraphs, ending. Use at least two contractions (I’m, we’ve, didn’t) because it is a friendly letter.
