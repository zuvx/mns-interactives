Key words — tap to open, then tap the speaker to hear the word
apostropheThe mark that shows letters are missing. Say it in parts: a / pos / tro / phe.
contractionTwo words joined and shortened: do not becomes don’t.
shortenTo make something shorter.
missingNot there any more. The apostrophe shows where letters went missing.
The key

One mark, one job

A contraction joins two words and throws some letters away. The apostrophe shows exactly where they went.

The rule. The first word usually stays whole. The letters come out of the second word, and the apostrophe stands in their place. do not loses the o and becomes don’t.
