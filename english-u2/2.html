pronounA word we use in place of a noun: he, she, it, they. Say it in parts: pro / noun.
singularOne of something. She is singular.
pluralMore than one. They is plural.
verbAn action word: run, write, send.
irregularA verb that changes shape in the past: go becomes went.
Message room

Shorter messages cost less

In a telegraph office every word costs money, so clerks say the same thing with fewer words. A pronoun does exactly that job: it stands in for a noun so you do not repeat it.

Listen to this. Maggie and Maggie’s family went up the tower. Maggie’s family waited in the lift. Now with pronouns: Maggie and her family went up the tower. They waited in the lift. Easier to read, and shorter.
