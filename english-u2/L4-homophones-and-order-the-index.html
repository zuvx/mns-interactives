The index room

Two jobs in this room

Every message here is filed a to z so a clerk can find it again. And every clerk spells carefully, because some words sound the same but mean different things.

Why it matters. Send meet you at four and your friend turns up. Send meat you at four and nobody knows what you mean. Same sound, different word.
