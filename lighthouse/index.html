The Lighthouse

Weeks 2 and 3 practice. Five rooms, one lamp to light.

Choose a room

Spell Beam

A word from the week 2 or week 3 spelling list. Green means the letter is in the right place. Yellow means it is in the word, but somewhere else.

Number Build

Say the number out loud first. What you say is what you build: hundreds, then tens, then ones.

The Stairs

Every step changes by the same amount. Work out what changes, then climb.

The Lamp Room

Open each door and answer what is inside. Every door gives you one number, and the four numbers light the lamp.

Tap the four numbers into the lock, in door order.

The Reading Room

Read the story, answer the questions, then write your own answer and compare it with a good one. Nobody marks the writing but you.
