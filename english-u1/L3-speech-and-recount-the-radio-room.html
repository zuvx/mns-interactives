Radio training

Welcome to the Radio Room

Speech marks go around the exact words somebody says. Nothing else goes inside them.

Alex shouted, “Hold on, Tom!”
The test. Could you hear those words if you were standing there? Then they go inside the marks. Alex shouted is not something you hear him say, so it stays outside.
