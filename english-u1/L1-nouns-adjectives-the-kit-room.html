Key words — tap to open, then tap the speaker to hear the word
nounA word that names a person, a place or a thing: helmet, cliff, Tom.
adjectiveA word that tells us more about a noun: a steep cliff.
dangerWhen something could hurt you. The g here is soft, like in giant.
terrifiedVery, very frightened. Say it in parts: terr / i / fied.
rescueTo save someone who is in danger. Say it in parts: res / cue.

Welcome to the Kit Room

Every piece of kit in this room has a name. A naming word is a noun. A word that tells us more about it is an adjective.

How this works. You do the thinking, the screen only checks. If you get something wrong you get a clue, and after two tries you get the answer explained. Nothing is ever taken off your points.
