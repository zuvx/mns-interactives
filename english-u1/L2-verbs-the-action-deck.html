Welcome to the Action Deck

A verb is what someone does: slipped, grabbed, flew. And these are verbs too: am, is, are, was, were — they tell us how someone is.

Watch out. Many people think a verb has to be something you can act out. I was in pain has a verb in it as well, and it is was.

The -ing machine

Add -ing to the verb

Most verbs: just add -ing look → looking · hold → holding
Ends in e: drop the e, add -ing hope → hoping · slide → sliding
Short word, one vowel, one last letter: double it run → running · grab → grabbing
