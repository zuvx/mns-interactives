Reporter training

Welcome to the Report Desk

Scanning is not reading every word. Find the keyword in the question, then hunt for that word in the report.

Step 1. Read the question twice.
Step 2. Pick the keyword: when, where, how many, a name.
Step 3. Run your finger down the report until you find it.
Note. The report below was written for this activity, so nobody in your class has read it before. Everything you need is in it.
The report

A busy afternoon for Green Lane fire station

Report from Green Lane fire station, Tuesday

The first call came in at half past two. A fire had started in the kitchen of a small cafe on Bridge Street. Six firefighters climbed into the engine and were on the road in ninety seconds.

When the crew arrived, thick smoke was pouring from the window. The cafe owner, Mrs Patel, had already brought everybody outside and shut the door behind her. "Shutting that door kept the fire in one room," said crew leader Sam Doyle.

The firefighters used one hose and two ladders. They put the fire out in twenty minutes. Nobody was hurt.

At four o'clock the same crew was called out again, this time to the river. A dog had run onto thin ice and could not get back. A firefighter in a bright orange suit crawled out on a board and carried the dog to the bank.

"People forget that we do far more than fires," said Sam Doyle. "Last month we rescued a cat, a horse and a lorry driver."
