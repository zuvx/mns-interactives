Upstairs

Line up the cakes

The bakers are lining up slices for the parade. Read the iced pieces from the bottom up and they get bigger. The unit fraction with the largest denominator is the smallest.

One piece each

Order the unit fractions

Drag each card into the line, or tap a card and then a gap. Check which end is smallest first.

Same pieces

Same denominator

The denominators are the same, so every piece is the same size. Look at how many pieces.

Number line

Find their place on the line

The line goes from 0 to 1 in eighths. Drag each card to its mark. If a card is not in eighths, find the same amount in eighths first.
